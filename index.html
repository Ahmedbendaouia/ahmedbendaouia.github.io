Associate Research Scientist · Institute for Advanced Manufacturing, UTRGV

Ahmed Bendaouia (Ben), Ph.D.

Minerals Processing & AI for Flotation  ·  Machine Learning / Deep Learning  ·  Digital Twins  ·  Advanced Manufacturing

I'm a research scientist at IAM, UTRGV in Texas, specializing in machine learning, computer vision, digital twins, and data-driven intelligent systems for large-scale physical and engineering applications. My work spans minerals processing and flotation monitoring, scalable data pipelines, multimodal data integration, predictive modeling, surrogate modeling, and AI-enabled workflow design for battery energy systems, advanced manufacturing, robotics, and critical minerals operations.

I develop deployable AI systems that integrate machine learning, data engineering, multimodal sensing, and process knowledge to support scientific discovery and operational decision-making. My current interests include multimodal data management for AI, large-scale training and inference workflows, MLOps in engineering systems, and agentic orchestration for data-intensive applications.

I completed my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence for Minerals Processing at Cadi Ayyad University in 2024 (Top 5% worldwide), conducted at MAScIR. Prior to my current role, I worked with Reminex, MANAGEM Group for three years developing and deploying AI-based solutions for operational excellence in mining, and earlier as a Data Scientist at LIUPPA, UPPA in France.

Beyond research, I co-founded several initiatives including IT Club, Together We Can, and Net3awnou, and served as an international ambassador for Youth for Human Rights International.

Peer-Reviewed Journals

Digital Twin for Battery Energy Storage Systems

Ahmed Bendaouia, Fenfen Wang, Hafsaa Ouifak, Jianzhi Li.

Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews, Volume 226, 2026.

IF 16.8 DOI: 10.1016/j.rser.2025.116347 →
Abstract

Proposes a comprehensive digital twin framework for Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) integrating real-time monitoring, predictive state estimation of State of Charge (SOC) and State of Health (SOH), and data-driven modeling to support manufacturing quality assurance and operational reliability. The framework bridges physical BESS dynamics with machine learning-based surrogate models, enabling adaptive control and predictive maintenance for energy storage applications.

Reinforcement Learning for Imbalanced Data in Robotic Anomaly Detection within Autonomous Manufacturing

Salma Messaoudi, Ahmed Bendaouia, El Hassan Abdelwahed, Mohammed Ameksa, Hajar Mousannif, Jianzhi Li.

SN Computer Science, 2026.

IF 2.4 DOI: 10.1007/s42979-026-04858-0 →
Abstract

Presents a reinforcement learning framework using Deep Q-Network (DQN) enhanced with LSTM and Gradient Boosting Machine (GBM) for anomaly detection in industrial robots, addressing class imbalance and temporal dependencies in sensor data. A novel GBM-based reward mechanism incorporating SMOTE integration achieves superior precision, recall, and F1-score compared to alternative DQN variants, enabling more resilient and autonomous manufacturing operations.

Intelligent Real-Time Flotation Froth Stability Monitoring Using Embedded Computer Vision

Ahmed Bendaouia et al.

International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology, 2025.

IF 3.72 DOI: 10.1007/s00170-025-16740-z →
Abstract

Develops a real-time froth stability monitoring system for lead (Pb) flotation processing by analyzing bubble dynamics—size, count, and spatial distribution—using embedded computer vision deployed at the industrial plant level. The system achieves over 97% accuracy, demonstrating the feasibility of edge-deployed AI for continuous froth stability assessment in mineral processing operations.

Artificial Intelligence for Enhanced Flotation Monitoring in Mining Industry

Ahmed Bendaouia et al.

Computers and Chemical Engineering, 2024.

IF 5 DOI: 10.1016/j.compchemeng.2023.108476 →
Abstract

Proposes a ConvLSTM-based approach for monitoring chemical composition in flotation froth during mining operations, effectively extracting spatial and temporal patterns from video data to enable real-time froth quality monitoring and process control. The model provides a deployable soft-sensing solution that enhances operational efficiency and reduces manual sampling in mineral recovery circuits.

Hybrid Feature Extraction for Online Mineral Grade Determination in Flotation Froth

Ahmed Bendaouia et al.

Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, 2024.

IF 8 DOI: 10.1016/j.engappai.2023.107680 →
Abstract

Applies image processing and deep learning to monitor mineral concentrate grades in flotation froth, analyzing texture, bubble size, velocity, and color distribution using convolutional neural networks. Achieves up to 94% accuracy in predicting zinc, lead, iron, and copper concentrate grades, demonstrating a viable AI-powered online analyzer for complex polymetallic flotation operations.

Advanced Spatio-Temporal Froth Analysis Using Smart Soft Sensors

Ahmed Bendaouia et al.

SN Computer Science, 2024.

IF 2.4 DOI: 10.1007/s42979-024-02706-7 →
Abstract

Develops smart soft sensors combining spatio-temporal deep learning models for online froth analysis in flotation circuits, enabling advanced real-time monitoring of mineral grade and process state. The approach integrates spatial feature extraction with temporal sequence modeling to capture the evolving dynamics of flotation froth for continuous process optimization.

Data-Driven Monitoring and Optimization of Froth Flotation Circuits

Oussama Hasidi et al., Ahmed Bendaouia.

Journal of Process Control, Volume 137, 2024.

IF 3.9 DOI: 10.1016/j.jprocont.2024.103198 →
Abstract

Presents an AI-based system for intelligent monitoring and optimization of froth flotation circuits using artificial neural networks and genetic algorithms, achieving improved process performance and recovery efficiency for mineral operations. The data-driven framework enables real-time adaptive control by learning complex relationships between process variables and flotation outcomes.

Digital Twin of Mineral Processing Operations for Advanced Monitoring

Oussama Hasidi et al., Ahmed Bendaouia.

International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology, 2024.

IF 3.72 DOI: 10.1007/s00170-024-13384-3 →
Abstract

Develops a data-driven digital twin for froth flotation operations using artificial neural networks trained on industrial and simulation data, achieving 94% accuracy in estimating flotation cell performance with a 2-second response time. The framework demonstrates how digital twins can revolutionize process monitoring, control, and efficiency in mineral processing industries.

Conv-LSTM for Real-Time Monitoring of Mineral Grades in Flotation Froth

Ahmed Bendaouia, El Hassan Abdelwahed, Sara Qassimi, Abdelmalek Boussetta, Intissar Benzakour, Oumkeltoum Amar, François Bourzeix, Achraf Soulala.

DATA 2023, Rome, Italy.

🏆 Best Paper Award
Abstract

Introduces a Convolutional LSTM model for real-time monitoring of mineral grades in flotation froth, capturing both spatial texture features and temporal dynamics from froth video sequences. The model achieves a mean absolute error of 4.52 in zinc differential flotation circuits, demonstrating strong potential for deployable online soft sensing in mineral processing plants.

Data-Driven and Model-Driven Approaches in Predictive Modeling for Operational Efficiency: Mining Industry Use Case

Oussama Hasidi, El Hassan Abdelwahed, et al., Ahmed Bendaouia.

MEDI 2023, Tunisia.

🏆 Best Paper Award
Abstract

Compares and combines data-driven and model-driven predictive modeling approaches for operational efficiency in the mining industry, providing a framework for selecting and integrating methods based on available data and process knowledge. The study demonstrates improved prediction performance when hybrid models leverage complementary strengths of physical constraints and machine learning flexibility.

Fourier Transform and Machine Learning for Real-Time Monitoring of Froth Flotation in Mining Industry

Ahmed Bendaouia, El Hassan Abdelwahed, Sara Qassimi, Abdelmalek Boussetta, et al.

IEEE CAIMP 2023, Agadir, Morocco.

🏆 Best Paper Award
Abstract

Combines Fourier transform-based frequency-domain feature extraction with machine learning classifiers for real-time monitoring of froth flotation in the mining industry, extracting periodic patterns from froth dynamics that are not captured by spatial features alone. The approach improves grade estimation accuracy and demonstrates the value of signal processing priors in industrial AI systems.
