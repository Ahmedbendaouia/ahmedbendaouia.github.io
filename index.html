About

AI that meets the physical world

I am an Assistant Professor of Research in Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Manufacturing in the Department of Manufacturing and Industrial Engineering and the Institute for Advanced Manufacturing, University of Texas Rio Grande Valley.

My work integrates machine learning, computer vision, and physics-informed modeling with real-world sensing, robotics, and decision-making. Across autonomous robotic systems, AI-enabled manufacturing and autonomous laboratories, and critical minerals and energy systems, the same hard problems recur: uncertainty, environmental drift, sensor fusion, physical constraints, reliability, and real-time decisions.

I lead and support multidisciplinary, externally funded research, proposal development, industry partnerships, R&D business incubation, and student mentoring. I earned my Ph.D. in AI for critical minerals separation at Cadi Ayyad University (2024, highly honorable distinction), in industrial collaboration with MANAGEM Group / Reminex.

Research Program

Five fronts, one question: can autonomy be trusted in the physical world?

Physical AI for Autonomous Robotic Systems

Perception, obstacle detection, and battery-aware mission supervision for autonomous maritime platforms such as the HydroCat surface vessel; ML-based reliability and anomaly detection for industrial robots.

  • Computer vision
  • Collision avoidance
  • Robot anomaly detection
  • RL

Autonomous Experimentation & AI Laboratories

Closed-loop workflows connecting data acquisition, predictive models, decisions, and robotic actions — with active learning, physics-informed models, and agentic AI for experiment orchestration.

  • Active learning
  • Agentic AI
  • Self-driving labs

AI & Digital Twins for Advanced Manufacturing

Real-time porosity monitoring in laser powder bed fusion, springback control in robotic sheet-metal bending, casting solidification monitoring, and interactive digital twins across LPBF, LDED, and WAAM.

  • LPBF
  • Thermal & machine vision
  • Physics-informed ML

AI for Critical Minerals & Physicochemical Systems

Industrial computer vision for froth flotation, ConvLSTM and vision-transformer soft sensors for online grade estimation, adaptive transfer learning under ore variability, and digital twins for mineral processing.

  • ConvLSTM
  • Soft sensors
  • Transfer learning
  • Edge AI

Digital Twins for Battery Energy Storage

Layered digital twin architectures for BESS, AI-based estimation of state of charge, health, and energy, anomaly detection, and energy resilience for autonomous platforms and the Rio Grande Valley.

  • SOC / SOH
  • Energy resilience
  • Uncertainty-aware
